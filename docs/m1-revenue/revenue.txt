# Revenue & Transaction Volume Pipeline Documentation

Technical documentation covering the data models, financial business logic, and automated quality gates for the revenue pipeline.

---

## 1. Overview

* **Objective:** Measure total monetary flow through the platform, evaluate category-level revenue contributions, and track transaction performance over time.
* **Source Data:** Transaction records from `raw.transactions` and merchant metadata from `raw.merchants`.
* **Orchestration:** Airflow DAG `revenue_pipeline` enforcing idempotency through a `TRUNCATE + INSERT` pattern.

---

## 2. ClickHouse Data Models

The analytics layer is organized across three ClickHouse tables in the `analytics` database:

### A. Transaction Fact Model (`analytics.transaction_facts`)
* **Grain:** One row per individual transaction.
* **Engine & Sorting Key:** `MergeTree()` ordered by `(transaction_date, merchant_id, transaction_id)`.
* **Key Features:** Status values normalized to lowercase, dates parsed to `Date` types, and `LowCardinality(String)` applied to status and category columns to optimize storage footprint and query execution.

### B. Daily Revenue Aggregate (`analytics.daily_revenue`)
* **Grain:** One row per calendar day.
* **Engine & Sorting Key:** `MergeTree()` ordered by `transaction_date`.
* **Metrics:** Overall transaction count, status-specific counts, completed gross revenue, refunded amount, failed volume, net revenue flow, and average order values.

### C. Category Revenue Aggregate (`analytics.category_revenue`)
* **Grain:** One row per calendar day per merchant category.
* **Engine & Sorting Key:** `MergeTree()` ordered by `(transaction_date, merchant_category)`.
* **Metrics:** Symmetrical to `analytics.daily_revenue` with an added `merchant_category` dimension for cross-category benchmarking.

---

## 3. Business Logic & Status Treatment

* **Completed Revenue (Gross):** Total monetary value of successfully settled transactions (`status = 'completed'`).
* **Refunded Amount:** Total monetary value of reversed transactions (`status = 'refunded'`).
* **Net Revenue:** Inflow remaining after subtracting refunds:
  `net_revenue = completed_revenue - refunded_amount`
* **Failed Amount:** Monetary value of failed payments (`status = 'failed'`); excluded from recognized revenue and monitored strictly as an indicator of lost sales opportunity.
* **Transaction Counts:**
  * `total_transaction_count`: Total transaction attempts across all statuses via `count()`.
  * `completed_count`, `failed_count`, and `refunded_count`: Segmented counts per status.
* **Averages:**
  * `avg_completed_amount`: Average Order Value (AOV) calculated exclusively on completed transactions.
  * `avg_refunded_amount`: Average amount across refunded items to monitor ticket-size refund exposure.

---

## 4. Data Quality & Validation

The automated Airflow task `validate_revenue` executes four data quality checks, halting the pipeline upon any discrepancy:

1. **Row Count Match:** Verifies that row counts in `analytics.transaction_facts` strictly match source records in `raw.transactions`.
2. **Dimension Completeness:** Confirms zero transactions have empty or unmapped merchant categories (`missing_count == 0`).
3. **Gross Revenue Reconciliation:** Ensures total completed revenue in `analytics.transaction_facts` matches `raw.transactions` within a 0.01 tolerance.
4. **Cross-Table Consistency:** Validates that daily totals for completed revenue, net revenue, and transaction counts in `analytics.daily_revenue` equal the sum of category aggregates in `analytics.category_revenue`.
